# Visual audit, second pass

A second audit of how the game looks, moves and reads, made 22 October 2026, the day after every stream of the [first visual audit](visual-audit.md) was marked done. Six auditors each took one area (the interface, its motion, items and the environment, people and their animation, the scene's effects and light, and the icon set) and judged the game as it is, not as it was planned. They played busy centers: the playtest bot built each campaign center to day 180 and day 540, and every picture was taken from those saves on the GPU at 1440×900 and other window sizes and pixel ratios. Each auditor's top findings were checked again against their pictures and the code; every P1 below was confirmed.

Follow-up, as of 25 October 2026: every stream was merged, in 0.33.0 to 0.33.4, though not every finding was fixed. A (0.33.3), C and F (0.33.0) and G (0.33.4) are done. B (0.33.3) did its P1s and part of the interface's P2s; still open are white on teal below AA, a muted color, the Books as one table, buttons and tabs from the keyboard, and a reduce-motion setting of the game's own. E (0.33.4) did its P1s and most of its P2s, but planting the feet was taken back out when the owner found the walk too intense, so feet still slide. D (0.33.0) made the mishaps, the progress bars, the weather, the day's light and the trees tell the truth; the rest of it, the room lamps' shapes among them, was dropped by the owner on 24 October 2026. Willow Lane's high street came in 0.33.5.

Severities: **P1** hurts reading the game or looks broken, **P2** is a clearly noticeable polish gap, **P3** is a nit.

## The short version

The clay art, the palette and the HUD's look are strong, and the first audit's streams held up. What is weak is how the game says "act now", the thing "challenging but fair" depends on most. Four patterns run through all six areas:

1. **Warnings reach the player worse than anything else.** Alarm bubbles look like small talk, patience shows only at its last stage, warning toasts are evicted, expire while the game is paused or sit under the inspector, staff warnings are 1.8:1 contrast, mishaps stop items while the room lamps stay green, and the advisor flips between two notes several times a second.
2. **Things were judged at three times play size.** The model lab and the stored pictures show a person about three times as big as at the default zoom. On a 1× screen at the default distance (41) a person is 30 to 35 px tall, a bubble's pictogram about 12 px, and most symptoms, wall decor, litter, upgrade plates and room signs a few pixels. On a 2× screen they read, which is likely why this went unnoticed.
3. **Changes snap instead of moving.** Guests pop 0.6 m onto tables, walls swap on the first frame of a turn, toasts vanish without leaving, bubbles blink on and off, overlays and the zoom snap, and the camera's glides run at the frame rate.
4. **Things that should look different do not.** Two of the five centers were the same park, room trims collide in pairs, guests wear the staff's uniform colors, staff and guest markers differ only in color, and the Massage Suite and Recovery Room have the same sign.

Since this audit: **The Exchange has a downtown setting of its own** (branch `claude/exchange-downtown-9a488f`, in 0.33.0): paving, office blocks across the side streets, street trees in planters, lamp posts and a crossing.

## How it was looked at

- The playtest bot (`tests/balance/bot.ts`) played each campaign center for 180 and 540 days, and the game state was loaded into the game as an autosave. The 540-day saves have 12 to 15 rooms, 100 to 160 items and 40 to 50 people.
- A screenshot harness drove Chromium on the Metal GPU (headless Chromium falls back to SwiftShader at 0.3 frames a second unless `--use-angle=metal` is passed), at 1024×768, 1280×720, 1366×768, 1440×900, 1920×1080 and 2560×1440, at pixel ratios 1, 1.25, 1.5 and 2, and camera distances from 15 to 78.
- Motion was read from bursts of frames and from a `requestAnimationFrame` stepper at fixed frame times; foot slip was measured from the feet's world positions frame by frame.
- Color blindness was simulated (Machado 2009 matrices).

Not checked: real Windows rendering at 125% and 150% (emulated on macOS), weaker GPUs, a machine room in a live save (none of the bot's saves built one), a sound bath in progress and most Shaman ceremonies, sound, and real color-blind players.

**Cost.** The 540-day Orchard draws about 1,460 calls and 1.75 million triangles a frame, 1.35 million of them in 52 people (about 26,000 each) and 690,000 in items. It holds 60 frames a second on an M4 Max. The perf test budgets fewer than 200 calls for a small center, so nothing measures a late game; a mid-range Windows laptop may stutter exactly when the animation matters most.

## P1 findings

### Warnings and alarms

**1. Alarm bubbles look and move like routine ones.** A guest about to walk out (`bubble-impatient`) or a member of staff about to quit (`bubble-quitting`) has the same white pin with a navy rim, the same size and the same 4 cm bob as "on break", "training" or "shopping". Only the pictogram differs, and at play size that is about 12 px. The impatient hourglass's red is a 4-unit cap inside a 3-unit navy stroke, so about one unit of red is left: none at 20 px and under, a sliver in the scene. Every bubble appears and disappears in a single frame. `scripts/icons.mjs:221` (`BUBBLE`, one look for every bubble), `src/render/pose.ts:743-763`, `src/render/models/character.ts:242`, `src/ui/icons/small/bubble-impatient.svg`. Fix: an alarm variant in the atlas (a red rim or red bubble with a white pictogram) for quitting, impatient and hurt, and an amber need variant for restroom, thirsty and exhausted; a gentle pulse on alarms (scale 1.0 to 1.15 at about 1.5 Hz); a 150 ms pop in and 100 ms shrink out; and the hourglass's sand as a solid red fill.

**2. Scene icons are too small on a 1× screen at the default zoom.** At distance 41 a bubble is 20.5 px tall with a 12 px pictogram, a use-square marker 19 px with a 9 px role pictogram; at the furthest zoom a bubble is 13 px. Cookie, cup, mug and gamepad are blobs. On a 2× screen the same view reads well. `character.ts:242` (0.62 m bubble), `src/render/overlays.ts:257` (0.62 m), `src/render/items.ts:298` (0.7 m); the art fills 76 of a bubble's 128 px cell (`scripts/icons.mjs:230`, `PLACING.bubble`). Fix: a floor on the size on screen (scale by `max(1, k·dist/41)`, or about 28 px for bubbles and 24 px for markers), and let the pictogram fill 88 to 92 of the cell.

**3. The advisor flips between notes several times a second.** On the 540-day Orchard at speed 3 its note changed 90 times in a minute, 184 at the Boardwalk, alternating between the Shop Counter note and "1 staffed treatment station draws no guests" every 0.2 to 1.5 s. The box jumps between 60, 97 and 133 px tall, and the figures inside a note change on every redraw. `src/ui/components/Advisor.tsx:14` calls `sim.adviceNote()` on every redraw (at least five a second, `src/app/refresh.ts:4`), and `advise()` in `src/core/sim/advisor.ts:77` has no memory. Fix: hold the shown note for about 8 s of real time or until its subject resolves, let only a higher class of advice replace it sooner, and hold its figures while it shows.

**4. Toasts lose warnings.** A fifth toast evicts the oldest whatever it is: "Percy Calmley, waiting for shamanism, is getting worse" was evicted after 5 s by "Ingrid Achewood thinks they are worth $3,920". Toasts expire after 6.5 s of real time, warnings too, and while the game is paused. At 20 a minute on a busy center at speed 3 a warning rarely lasts. The room inspector covers the toast column, raise requests pile up behind it with only their gold edges showing, and the overlay picker hides the column whole. `src/ui/hud.ts:80` (`MAX_TOASTS`), `hud.ts:320-340`; `Toasts.css:3-10`, `Inspector.css:7-11`, `OverlayPicker.css:3-9`. Fix: evict info and good toasts before bad ones; keep a bad toast about someone for about 12 s or until clicked; stop the clocks while paused, while a dialog is open and while the pointer is over the stack; put the toasts and the inspector in one right-hand column; fold raise requests into one pill; animate eviction with the fade, and slide the stack rather than jump it.

**5. Staff and item warnings are the hardest text in the game to read.** "Miserable: will quit in 9 days" and the ZenMart offer are `#a8322b` on the navy status pill, 1.81:1; "To be promoted, fill every qualification slot" is `#8a5a12` on navy, 2.04:1. Broken, filthy, smoking, flooded and unstaffed all use the same navy pill as "Staffed by…", told apart by a small icon. `src/ui/components/inspector/Inspector.css:62-68` and `:156-170` (`.status.needs` and `.status.quitting` change the text color only); `ItemInfo.tsx:49, 72, 123, 250-282`; `StationInfo.tsx:102-131`; `RoomInfo.tsx:122` uses a `status flooded` class with no CSS. Fix: tones for status lines, `.status.bad` (light red, dark red text), `.status.warn` (cream gold) and navy for neutral, reusing the `.status.quitting.alarm` look (`#fbe3e1`, 5.4:1).

**6. Mishaps stop items, and the scene shows none of it.** Wasps at the Orchard change nothing visible. In a freeze at High Pines only the radiators' heat lines stop: toilets, sinks, tea and fountains look normal and the Restroom's lamp stays green. In a power cut the warming stones keep glowing, the snack machine stays lit and every lamp stays green. `src/render/room-lamp.ts:33-58` (`lampStates`) checks only wear and flood water and never `world.stoppedByMishap`; `items.ts:76, 100` (`glow`, `led`) and `item-cues.ts:401` (`wearLight`) ignore it. Fix: a `stopped` condition for cues read from `world.stoppedByMishap`, stopped lamps, LEDs and glows dark, a stopped station counted in `lampStates` (or a blue-grey "stopped" lamp), and one signature effect per mishap: buzzing dots over stopped snacks, drinks and bins for wasps, a frost tint on stopped water items for the freeze.

**7. Staff and guests are told apart by color alone.** `marker-staff` and `marker-guest` are the same figure, teal or orange, and under deuteranopia and protanopia two near-equal olives. A staffed use square is purple (`#9b6bff`, `overlays.ts:48`) but carries an orange role marker, the same orange as the guest marker. And guest tops (`src/core/people.ts:31-33`) include the receptionist's teal (`0x2a9d8f` against `0x2fa8a0`), the consultant's and practitioner's white, the healer's blue and the Caretaker's sage, so a receptionist does not stand out at reception; the lab's guest (`LAB_LOOK.top`, `src/lab/people.ts:12`) is in the receptionist's teal. Fix: a different silhouette for the guest marker; role markers on a purple disc, or staff squares in the staff marker's teal; guest tops no uniform uses (swap values, not the list's length, so the random stream is unchanged, and remap stored colors in a save upgrade).

### The scene and animation

**8. Progress bars vanish on three of the four camera turns.** A treatment's bar is turned to face the camera once, at 45° (`src/render/items.ts:318`), and never again: at turns 1 and 3 it is edge-on, a one-pixel sliver, and at turn 2 its back faces the camera and it fills from the right. It is the scene's only sign that a treatment is under way. Fix: set the bar's angle from the turn in `ItemsLayer.setTurn` and on each new bar, or copy the camera's rotation each frame; add a picture at turn 1 to the visual tests.

**9. Every placement reads as a failure.** On the frame after an item is placed, the next item's ghost sits red on top of it ("Something is in the way"); after a door click the table's ghost is red under the pointer ("Keep doorways and access spots clear"). The pointer has not moved, so a player sees this on every placement. A refused click changes nothing on screen (only a sound), a ghost is green for an item the bank cannot pay for, and buying shows no money floating off though a refund does. `src/app/tools/tools.ts:365-450` (`pointerUp`), `:507-537` (`drawItem`), `:149` (`refuse()`), `:154-157` (cost checked after the click); `src/core/sim/building.ts:541`. Fix: hide or grey the ghost and the problem line until the pointer moves a few pixels or 250 ms pass; settle a new item with a 150 ms squash; nudge the ghost and pulse the problem line on a refusal; check the cost in `drawItem`; float the price spent.

**10. Guests walk into the table, pop 0.6 m onto it, and drop through it to get off.** On arrival the guest walks into the massage table at floor height, legs in the frame, then in one frame stands upright on the tabletop and tips over in about 0.2 s; getting off, they drop to the floor still lying flat, head out of the table's end, and stand up inside it. It is the most repeated moment in the game, at both ends of every treatment. `src/core/sim/movement.ts:44-47` and `:66-71` set the pose, height and facing in one tick on the last waypoint, which is the lying spot on the table itself; `src/render/pose.ts:306-307` sets position and facing outright. Fix: ease the shown height and facing over about 0.3 s in the renderer; better, end the walk at the access tile and play a short climb, and the reverse when getting up.

**11. Most symptoms cannot be seen at play zoom.** Of 62 conditions, about 35 do not read at distance 41 without the inspector: slump, neck, crick, knotted, steam, frost, dizzy, pins, bottled, cold feet, drained, static, scattered, dim, tune, noisy, out of body, far out, earworm, bad vibrations, glass half, monotone, spirit animal, toxic ex, lost, blocked, tilted, sea legs, wipeout, cabin, blossom, chair shaped, blue screen, salted, washed up, echo, past life, bad apple and old soul. The knot sits at the back of the torso (`character.ts:248-256`), hidden whenever the guest faces the camera or sits. The chicken, the game's signature, has no scale-up (`character.ts:559-574`) while the gull is ×1.7, the cactus ×1.6 and the compass ×1.5. Fix: about ×1.6 on every prop hung from the head or torso, the knot on the shoulder, the hen at ×1.7, a prop as well as a pose for the posture conditions, and a visual test of every condition at distance 41 at 1:1.

### Interface layout

**12. In a late game the bottom bar covers the inspector and menus at 1440×900.** With a Year 2 summer date and a six-figure balance the stats bar moves up a row, but the inspector is fixed at `bottom: 104px` (`Inspector.css:9`), so its last rows ("Can deliver", "Hot Stone") run under the bar, as do the bottom right corners of the menus. The stored pictures, a Year 1 winter game with $76,580, never reach it. `Hud.css:6-15`, `tokens.css:24-32` (`--bar-h` fixed at 104 and 164). Fix: set `--bar-h` from a ResizeObserver on the bar and use it for the inspector; shorten the date ("Summer · M7 Y2", the whole date in the tooltip); add a visual test with a Year 2 summer save.

**13. Below 1430 px the date cell is hidden, and with it "Rush hour", "Snowed in", the temperature and the season.** 1366 and 1280 are the commonest laptop widths, and at 1366 about 125 px of the row is still empty. `StatsBar.css:84-88`. Fix: a compact cell (season icon, day, temperature, the rush or snow chip when it applies) rather than none.

## P2 findings

### Interface

- **White on teal, green, orange and cyan is below AA.** Round-button labels are 11 px white on `#2fa8a0` (2.90:1, 2.31 on hover); the selected tab 2.90; "go" buttons white on `#3bb273` (2.69); the orange "on" state 2.49; the title screen's menu white on `#19bcd6` (2.28). `buttons.css:9-12, 26, 39, 184-187`, `Menu.css:156-159`, `StatsBar.css:72-75`, `TitleScreen.css`. Fix: fill with `--teal-dark` `#23827c` (4.61) or put navy text on the teal (4.16); darken "go" to about `#23805a` and "on" to about `#c9622a`; move the title screen onto the game's teal.
- **Opacity stands in for a muted color,** so live controls look disabled and small text drops under 3:1: the sandbox's difficulty labels end at 0.56 opacity (`.settings li span` applies twice), the Staff menu's gold "morale" key is 2.13:1, locked cards about 2.2:1, and 22 rules put 0.5 to 0.6 opacity on 11 to 12 px text. `SettingsPanel.css:47-50`, `DifficultySettings.tsx:45-47`, `StaffMenu.css:37-62`, `Menu.css:108-112`. Fix: a `--muted` token (`#5b6b7a`, about 5:1).
- **The room inspector opens with generic copy.** Prestige's four-line explanation, comfort, draw and bad vibes all come before the staffing line; "Needs a Acupuncturist" sits 60% of the way down above six outline buttons. `RoomInfo.tsx:150-200`. Fix: staffing and warnings first, then the line and how busy it is; the prestige explanation into a tooltip; Move, Resize, Door, Windows, Copy and Template as an icon row.
- **Status colors do not mean one thing.** Gold is a warning (payroll, challenge, requests) and also "All in order" in the inspection report (`VisitDialog.css:23-26`); the Stats charts draw "Stormed out" and "Put off by prices" green like "Healed"; every $0 row in the Books is green or red (`MoneyMenu.tsx:30`); side income lines are teal like links (`MoneyMenu.css:200-202`). Fix: `--good`, `--warn`, `--bad` and `--neutral` tokens, a polarity per chart, zero rows muted.
- **The Books tab is hard to compare.** Two lists repeat the same 13 labels, so a line's two months are 340 px apart; the net chart has no zero line, months or values. Fix: one table (line, this month, last month, change), only the totals colored, month initials and a baseline on the chart.
- **Bars run in both directions and turn red at a cliff.** Condition and patience fill green as they improve, thirst, hunger and bladder orange as they worsen; no bar has a value; need bars never turn red; patience goes green to red at 25. `GuestInfo.tsx:121, 190-211`, `StaffInfo.tsx:189-202`. Fix: an amber stage, need bars red near full, the value shown.
- **Advisor notes run to six or nine lines,** about 410×170 px over the scene (`Advisor.css:3-9`). Fix: the first sentence as a bold headline, the rest clamped to two lines, all of it on hover or click.
- **The toast stack repeats itself.** A raise shows as a lasting pill and as a toast; the " ›" link marker wraps onto a line of its own (`Advisor.css:47-52`); there is no log of past messages.
- **The overlay key covers the Turn button** (`OverlayPicker.css:48-55` sets `right: 232px`; the system buttons need 266) and runs into announcements (`Announcement.css:3-8`).
- **Menus are big and fixed, and their headers scroll away.** At 1280×720 the Items menu shows three rows of filter tabs and a row and a half of cards; the Items menu scrolls 3,474 px; the place and kind filter rows look alike when selected. `Menu.css:3-12, 140-169`. Fix: sticky title and tabs, the place filter as one dropdown chip.
- **The Staff list cannot be scanned for trouble:** 17 rows with no role, rank or flag for a raise, a rival offer, quitting or a course, and no sorting (`StaffMenu.tsx:26-60`).
- **The type scale is too fine:** 19 font sizes, 13 of them between 11 and 15.5 px in half-pixel steps, weight 600 used 86 times, a menu title (17/600) barely above a card title (15/600). Fix: six sizes (11.5, 13, 14.5, 17, 21, 28) and three weights.
- **Keyboard.** Enter and Space do nothing on a focused button (`common/Button.tsx:33-48` handles `onPointerDown` only), the focus ring is the browser's thin default with no `:focus-visible` rule anywhere, and the in-game tabs have no `role="tab"` (the campaign's do, `CampaignPanel.tsx:39-44`).
- **On every build button, half the icon disappears.** At rest the teal parts vanish into the teal button (the bodies in `ui-staff` and `ui-hire`); when on, the orange parts vanish into the orange (the Rooms floor, the Items pot, the Hire head, the Staff heads, the Treatments petal): 1.0 to 1.2:1, the navy outline alone carrying the shape. `BuildButtons.tsx:82, 97`, `buttons.css:30-75`. Fix: the icon on a cream inner disc, as the system buttons have.

### Motion and feedback

- **The camera's glide and turn run at the frame rate:** a quarter turn takes 1000, 500 and 208 ms at 30, 60 and 144 Hz, a 15-tile glide 1133, 567 and 236 ms. `src/render/camera.ts:86` (`lerp 0.18` a frame), `camera-math.ts:27` (`TURN_GLIDE 0.2` a frame). Everything else already uses the time step. Fix: `k = 1 - exp(-rate·dt)` at about 12 a second for the glide and 13 for the turn.
- **Clicking a toast snaps the zoom from 41 to 26 on the first frame** while the target glides over 590 ms (`camera.ts:47-51`). Fix: a goal for the distance, eased with the target over about 450 ms.
- **A turn swaps every wall on its first frame:** at a fifth of the way round the walls are already cut for the end view (`src/render/view.ts:163-170`). Fix: swap at halfway, or ease the walls' heights through the turn.
- **Event announcements are overwritten by jokes:** at speed 3, six of eight announcements replaced one still showing, once after 0.58 s, and "Emergency incoming!" was replaced within 2 s by a quip. A new line swaps in place with no entrance. `hud.ts:347-358`, `Announcement.tsx:13`. Fix: a queue, at least 3 s for an event line, flavor lines dropped while something shows, a key per line.
- **A new pill pushes the open emergency offer down under the pointer:** a bad trip starting while an emergency offer is open moves Accept from y≈160 to y≈342 in one frame (`EmergencyOffer.tsx:22-31`). Fix: new pills below an open offer.
- **Floating money is pinned to the screen and piles up:** it stays put while the camera pans 4 tiles, three at one spot smear, and a busy center makes 160 a minute, 12 at once. `hud.ts:315, 361-368`, `Floaters.css:11`, `global.css:106`. Fix: re-project the world point each frame, offset and merge neighbours, animate `transform`.
- **Speech bubbles flash by at speed 3:** a bubble's median life is 1.75 s at speed 1 and 0.73 s at speed 3 (2.2 sim-seconds, `src/core/sim/chars.ts:53`). Fix: a real-time minimum of about 1.4 s in the renderer.
- **Reduced motion is barely respected, and there is no setting.** With `prefers-reduced-motion: reduce` toasts still slide, panels and dialogs pop, money floats, a glide takes 33 frames and the chevron spins; only `tooltip.css:96` and `TitleScreen.css:375` check it. Fix: a global block in `global.css` turning pops and slides into fades, and a "Reduce motion" setting (defaulting from the system) that also cuts camera jumps and turns and stops the chevron, ring pulse and bubble bob.
- **Money and reputation change silently:** a $34,550 payday and −6 reputation land in one frame (`StatsBar.tsx:41-56`). Fix: count the number over 300 ms, flash the cell past a threshold, float a "−6" off reputation.
- **A selected room has no mark in the scene,** and the hover sheet is 0.15 white, hard to see on wood (`overlays.ts:181-186`; `updateRing`, `:285-311`, handles people and items only). Fix: a gold outline round the selected room's floor, the hover at about 0.25, faded in.

### People

- **Feet still slide, more at speeds 2 and 3.** The slower foot moves 0.38 m for each meter walked at speed 1, 0.50 at speed 2 and 0.60 at speed 3 (a planted foot would be near 0); the widest stride falls from 0.50 to 0.34 m. Every limb angle eases with `k = min(1, dt·14)` in real time (`pose.ts:685`), which filters the cycle: at speed 3 it keeps about 40% of the swing. And the swing is short: ±0.62 rad on a 0.42 m leg covers about 0.49 m a step, where `STRIDE = 4.8` (`pose.ts:135`) asks for 0.65. Fix: apply the walk cycle's angles directly while walking, easing only a blend weight; raise `STRIDE` to about 6.4 or the swing to about 0.85; measure until the slower foot moves under 0.1 m a meter.
- **People walk through each other:** a guest leaving the massage room through the therapist at the door, guests through each other at reception (three pairs 2 to 8 cm apart at once), a Caretaker and a guest in a doorway. Nothing separates people (`movement.ts` moves straight along paths; `people.ts` draws positions as they are). Fix: a small eased offset per person in the renderer, up to about 0.25 m, away from anyone within 0.45 m; the tables' staff spot out of the door's lane.
