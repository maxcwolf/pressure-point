Rare quirk props

Set aside for now, 27 October 2026. The props are not being built, and the mock code was dropped from the branch. Holds Space was changed instead, as fix A below and stronger: colleagues at work within 5 tiles, through walls, work 15% faster.

A rare quirk is the one thing about a person worth remembering, so it should show on the floor, not only in a gold box in the inspector. Each of the four gets one prop that is the joke on its chip, worn or held so you can pick that person out of a crowd, and one moment when the quirk visibly does its job. Nothing in the simulation changes: the props are drawn from the quirks a person already has.

Props are built as a mockup on the branch, drawn by the game’s own character model: open the game with ?props, or the model lab at /lab.html?model=staff:therapist and pick a rare quirk. The moments are described, not built. Game shots are the playtest bot’s Orchard Estate at day 500, with the four quirks given to four staff working near each other. Former Influencer: the phone

“Every guest they heal posts about it: +1 reputation each. Stops to film it: 10% slower.”

What it looks likeA black phone held up and out at eye height, the screen glowing blue toward their face, a bright flash dot toward the room.
When it showsStanding between guests: the 10% they lose is the filming. Put away the moment a guest lies down, and on the walk.
The momentEach heal, the flash pops and a small “+1 ★” rises off them, so you see the reputation arrive from that person.

Burnout Survivor: the mug

“Morale never falls below 35. Has a mug about it.”

What it looks likeA white mug with a red band and a handle, coffee in it, two wisps of steam, held at the chest with the elbow bent. The words on the mug are left to your imagination.
When it showsWhenever their hands are free, walking included: it goes everywhere with them. Set down while they treat.
The momentWhen their morale hits 35 and stops, a long sip and a bigger puff of steam. Everyone else would be heading for the door.

Healing Hands: the glow

“One treatment in ten that would have failed works anyway.”

What it looks likeHands lit warm from within, a soft gold halo round each, four motes of light circling it.
When it showsWhile treating, the halos pulse and the motes turn. Between guests a smaller, steady glow, so you can still find them.
The momentWhen the dice save a treatment, a gold burst over the guest, and the outcome line says “Saved by Healing Hands”, so the player learns why that one worked.

Holds Space: the scarf and the ring

“Colleagues working in the same room work 10% faster. Mentions it.”

What it looks likeA lavender scarf wrapped loosely round the neck, one end hanging down the front. The only lavender anyone wears.
When it showsAlways. The ring shows under every colleague they are speeding up, in the same lavender, so the link reads without a line.
The momentThe ring is the moment, and it is built in the mockup. It answers “who is this helping?” at a glance.

Holds Space, as built, almost never works

0 of 12

Building the ring turned this up. The rule on the branch is “colleagues posted in the same room and within 5 tiles”. But in the bot’s Orchard at day 500, all 12 staff at posts are alone in their rooms: one therapist per Massage Suite, one consultant per consulting room, and so on. Receptionists and caretakers have no room post at all. So a Holds Space hire would have sped up nobody, for a quarter more pay. A player who builds two tables in one room could make it work, but nothing tells them to.

★ Recommended

A. Nearby, any room

Colleagues at their posts within 5 tiles, wherever they are: Ray of Sunshine’s reach. In the same save, 8 of the 12 would have someone near enough. The mockup’s ring already uses this rule, which is why it shows in the pictures above.

Chip text: “Colleagues working nearby work 10% faster. Mentions it.” About an hour: one condition in heldSpace, its test, the guide line.

B. Same or next-door rooms

Closer to “holding the room”, and a reason to place rooms side by side. But the simulation does not know which rooms touch today, so it needs room adjacency worked out, kept up as rooms change, and explained.

About a day.

C. Everyone, a little

The whole center works 3% faster while they are on shift. Always on and easy to explain, but there is nothing to point at: no ring, no placement, and it reads as one more tiny buff.

About an hour.

Building it for real

  • Props: the mockup is about 130 lines in quirk-props.ts, drawn from a person’s quirks, with no save change. Firming it up (turned with the camera, hidden in the low-detail view, checked on every role’s pose) is about a day.
  • Moments: the floater, the gold burst, the sip and the outcome line are about a day and a half.
  • Holds Space: fix A first, before the props: about an hour.
  • Before building: the mug and the phone only just read at the default zoom. Making each about a third bigger, as the glow already was after the first shots, should be enough; the ring and the glow are fine as they are.
