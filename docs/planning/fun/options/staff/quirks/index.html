Every staff quirk

Sixteen quirks, as they are built on the branch: twelve common ones (seven that help, five that cost you) and four rare ones. Each card shows the chip as the game draws it, the words the game uses, the numbers behind them, what they do to pay, how often an applicant rolls them, and what you can see on the floor today. Where nothing shows yet, the card has an idea for what could.

The odds are from 100,000 rolls of the game’s own rollQuirks. “Floor idea” lines are proposals only; the rare quirks’ props are mocked up on their own page, Rare quirk props.

How an applicant gets them

1–3

common quirks each, a third of applicants with one, a third with two, a third with three. Nobody has none.

≤ 1

flaw each. 63% of applicants carry one red chip; nobody carries two.

1 in 7

also rolls a rare quirk, on top of the common ones: 3.6% for each of the four.

× pay

Each quirk multiplies the salary asked. A rare one asks a quarter more, whatever else they have.

Four pairs are opposites and never turn up on one person:

Brisk never with Chatterbox
Tireless never with Sleepyhead
Power Walker never with Dawdler
Grump never with Ray of Sunshine
