Pressure Point Institute · Fun gap 2 of the 27 October review

Symptoms you can see

In Two Point Hospital you read the waiting room from across the map: a cube, a clown, a mummy. Here most of the 62 ailments are a small prop on an ordinary person, a cork, a glass, a hen, so at the usual zoom, where a guest is about 33 pixels tall, a crowd of thirty reads as a crowd, and the jokes live in names nobody reads. Below are four ways to fix that, each tried for real on three ailments, and one recommendation.

How the pictures were made: a day-900 Orchard Estate built by the playtest bot (seed 2, machines on), loaded in the game at 1440×900 with the camera at its default distance over the busiest spot indoors. For each option, every unwell guest was given one of that option's three ailments, and the same save was shot as the game draws it today (before) and with the prototype on (after). Corridor pictures are shown at their real pixel size; click any picture for the whole screen. Close-ups are from the model lab at the game's camera angle.

A

The body becomes the joke

Recommended

Tried on Stress Head, Bottled Up, Scrambled Noggin

For the ailments whose name already names a thing, the guest turns into that thing: a stress head is a kettle, someone bottled up walks about inside a bottle, a scrambled noggin has an egg for a head.

On the floor
A new outline and a new color at head height or whole-body size. In the crop the green bottles, white eggs and black kettle handles can be picked out at 33 pixels without zooming in; today the same crowd shows a few specks of white and gold.
At the cure
The thing shrinks away in the puff and their own head, hair or body is back at the halfway mark, then the hop. Built in the prototype. There is room for more: the lid blows off, the cork pops, the shell cracks.
Work for the full set
About half a day per ailment with sitting, lying, treatments and the machine gags checked: ~25 days for all 62, ~5 for the first ten. About 20 ailments suit it; the rest can keep their props.
What could go wrong
A whole-body shape has to work lying on a table, sitting, in a queue and in every machine gag; the bottle goes round the body, and the prototype does not yet handle lying down. Hiding the hair must never hide the face: the kettle and the egg keep eyes, brows and mouth. Five looks are shared by two ailments each today (pale, dizzy, cloud, jitter, static), so those pairs need telling apart. Steer clear of Two Point's own shapes, such as a light bulb for a head.
B

The symptom is in how they move

Tried on Possessed by a Chicken, Cold Feet, Scrambled Noggin

Each ailment gets its own walk and idle: possessed by a chicken struts with elbows flapping and head pecking, cold feet high-step and hop from foot to foot, a scrambled noggin staggers with arms out.

On the floor
Motion carries at small sizes better than detail, and in the moving clip the strutting and staggering show. A still picture barely changes, and the outline is still a person.
At the cure
The walk straightens out on the spot, then the hop. A clear change for anyone watching that guest; nothing in a screenshot.
Work for the full set
About a third of a day per ailment, because each walk has to sit with queueing, sitting, carrying a drink and every mood: ~18–20 days for all 62.
What could go wrong
The game already uses body language for a guest's state: patience running out (stamping, folded arms, glances at the door), stress, the bad trip. A walk per ailment competes with all of it and makes mood harder to read, which the challenging-but-fair rule rules out unless the two are kept strictly apart. Many names have no walk in them (Bottled Up, Glass Half Empty).
C

Props that grow from afar

Tried on Bottled Up, Glass Half Empty, Flat Battery

A symptom prop is kept at least 18 pixels across however far out the camera is, as speech bubbles are, up to 2.6 times its own size.

On the floor
The cork, glass and battery become readable blobs over heads. They are the same props, so they read as what they are, but there is nothing new to laugh at.
At the cure
Unchanged: the bigger prop shrinks away in the same puff, which is easier to see.
Work for the full set
~2–3 days for all 62: one mount and one size per prop, done in one place.
What could go wrong
Grown props over a crowd pile up on each other and on the mood bubbles, which sit in the same space over the head and matter more. Their size changes with zoom, so the same guest looks different in every picture. It fixes legibility, not the fun.
D

Wear it as color

Tried on Out of Body, Cold Shoulder, Stagnant Vibes

The whole person takes on the ailment's color: out of body is a see-through ghost, a cold shoulder is frosted blue from head to toe, stagnant vibes are pond green with flies.

On the floor
Color reads at any distance: a frosted figure or a ghost stands out at once. The outline does not change, so it says "something is wrong with that one" more than what is wrong.
At the cure
The color drains back at the halfway mark of the puff, a good payoff.
Work for the full set
~4–6 days for all 62: a palette per ailment, checked in each center's light and weather.
What could go wrong
Clothes are how the player tells one guest from another, and the Condition overlay and heat map already use color for how unwell a guest is, so a third meaning for color muddies both. The ghost makes the face hard to read, so mood is harder to see. Most names have no color in them.

Recommendation

A: the body becomes the joke, for the ailments that name a thing

It is the only option that changes the outline, which is what Two Point's crowd is read by, and in the corridor it is the only one where the ailment can be named at play size rather than just noticed. It keeps the face, so a guest's mood reads as well as it does today, and it gives the cure a real payoff: the person comes back. Days are spent per ailment, so it can go out ten at a time.

The first ten, all found in every center, each a name that already is a picture, each treated in a different room, and all five disciplines among them:

  1. Stress Head: a whistling kettle (prototyped).
  2. Bottled Up: inside a big green bottle, the cork above the head (prototyped).
  3. Scrambled Noggin: a cracked egg for a head (prototyped).
  4. Flat Battery: the body a battery, legs out of the bottom, one red bar blinking.
  5. Spiritually Dehydrated: a cactus in a terracotta pot, prickles and all.
  6. Glass Half Empty: a tumbler, the water line at the waist.
  7. Scattered: in pieces, head, body and legs drifting a hand's width apart.
  8. Noisy Mind: a clockwork head with the gears turning behind glass.
  9. Lost Their Way: a signpost for a head, its arms pointing every way.
  10. Blocked Qi: head and shoulders lost in a small gray storm cloud.

The prototype is on the branch behind ?symptoms=body (and walk, grow, color) in the game's address, and &symptoms=body in the model lab. Without it nothing changes.
