# Possible additions

Ideas worth keeping that nobody has decided on yet. Nothing here is planned: an idea moves to [planning/todo.md](todo.md) or [planning/roadmap.md](roadmap.md) once it is chosen. Ideas for new items have their own list, in [planning/item-ideas.md](item-ideas.md).

## Rooms and treatments

- **A signature room at each center,** like Two Point Hospital's single-illness clinics: a room that cures one or two of the center's own conditions, with an animation of its own. For example a Float Tank at The Boardwalk, a Sauna at High Pines, or a cider-press bath at The Orchard Estate. It would give each center more character and add the room-fitting puzzle that much of Two Point's fun comes from. Raised 15 October 2026 when comparing our treatments with Two Point's. Worked up on 21 October 2026 as a machine for each center, in [planning/machine-ideas.md](machine-ideas.md), with a plan for building them in [plans/machines.md](../plans/machines.md). Built in 0.31.0, as a machine room at each campaign center.
- **Rooms that suit each other.** Today a room's neighbors only ever cost it, through noise. A few pairings would pay instead: a Recovery Lounge beside the Ceremony Lodge shortens the rest after a ceremony, and a Staff Room near the busiest suite shortens the walk to a break. They would show on the overlay while a room is placed, so the rules are in plain sight, unlike [Ley Lines](fun/README.md#wild-cards). It gives the layout puzzle, the game's strongest system, something to aim for as well as something to avoid. Raised 2 November 2026 in a review of what to build next.

## Stars and goals

- **Goals built around each center's twist.** Every star is drawn from the same nine or so kinds of goal, and none asks about a center's machine, its twist, its classes or its regulars ([fun/README.md](fun/README.md#where-the-fun-sags-today), gap 1). One goal a star could be written for the center instead: heal 20 guests during High Pines' freeze, a summer month at The Boardwalk with no guest going home worse off, three devoted regulars at The Orchard Estate. It is the companion to opening the third star's content at the second ([fun/grand-occasions.md](fun/grand-occasions.md)). Raised 2 November 2026 in a review of what to build next.

## Staff

- **A choice at each promotion.** At each new rank, the player picks one of two perks for the person promoted, each with a cost, so someone raised in the center becomes someone no applicant can replace. It answers the churn the bot finds best (404 swaps in 30 games, [balance/log.md](../balance/log.md), entry 47) without punishing letting someone go, and works alongside [Staff Room Politics](fun/staff-room-politics.md). The bot already keeps its practitioners, because their courses are an investment no applicant brings; it swaps Consultants and Receptionists, who have few courses, and mostly at the last two ranks. Raised 2 November 2026 in a review of what to build next, and written up the same day as [Promotion Perks](fun/promotion-perks.md), with every perk, its numbers and where it plugs in. Settled so far, on 2 November 2026:
  - **Two choices a career,** at the promotions to Senior and to Expert. The first two promotions stay one click.
  - **Senior is how they work:** a pair of trade-offs for each role, such as a Sound Healer's Big Gong (one more mat, louder) or Hushed (no noise leaves the room). **Expert is what they leave behind:** Mentor (colleagues of lower rank nearby learn faster, they work more slowly) or Name on the Door (guests they healed ask for them, wait longer and tip more, for touchier morale and bigger raises).
  - **A perk from a hire is rare:** about one Senior or Expert applicant in ten brings one. A hire above Experienced earns the choices their rank owes them by settling in, once they have earned in the center the experience each promotion takes. Someone raised here has their perk the day they are promoted.
  - **Promote all promotes now, and the choice waits** on a card for each one promoted.
  - **Every perk costs something,** and the bot is rerun: if perks make the game easier, the base comes down to match, as Staff Room Politics plans for morale.

## Business

- **Referral contracts.** A local firm offers a season of steady guests at a rate agreed up front, with a clause on how long they wait. At The Exchange, a bank's HR team might send six burnt-out analysts a week at 80% of the price; break the waiting clause twice and they cancel, and the Rag hears about it. It is a trade of steady money against space in the lines, an active way out of the mid-center squeeze, and a reason for a price to move. Unlike a house call, the guests come to the center, and the deal runs all season. Raised 2 November 2026 in a review of what to build next.

## Visitors

- **Inspectors with traits,** some more corrupt than others: one who takes almost any offer, one who takes none and fines you more for trying, and most in between, named on the notice when they write ahead so the player can weigh it before they call. It would make the offers to make a failed inspection go away a read of the inspector rather than fixed odds. Raised 1 November 2026 by Max while the three offers were built for 0.36.0, and left out of that release.
