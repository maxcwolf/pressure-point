# People pass: the clay people, moving as they should

A review, 22 October 2026, of the people in the scene: how they are built (`src/render/models/character.ts`, `character-detail.ts`, `character-machines.ts`), how they are posed each frame (`src/render/pose.ts`), how the crowd is drawn (`src/render/crowd.ts`, `people.ts`) and what the model lab shows of them (`src/lab/catalog.ts`, `src/lab/people.ts`). The yardstick is the one [visual-audit.md](visual-audit.md) set under Models: the people are the best asset in the game, and every change here should make them read better at the zoom most play happens at, without costing the crowd a draw call it does not need.

**The verdict.** The figures are finished: the fused shapes, the knees and elbows, the faces, the hair, the uniforms and the symptom props are all good and the lab pictures prove it. What is left is motion that does not match what the game is doing, and a crowd that is nine people wearing the same body. In order of how much a player sees it:

1. **Feet slide at double and triple speed.** The scene's clock is real seconds (`View.render` adds `dt` to `this.time`) while the simulation runs at `dt × s.speed` (`Sim.tick`), and `poseChar` swings the legs from `t × 9.5`. At the speeds the game is mostly played at, people glide across the floor with their legs going at walking pace; a runner at `RUNAWAY_SPEED` 3.2 and a stiff guest at 1.75 share one cadence with everyone at 2.1.
2. **Everyone busy does a massage.** `poseChar`'s last `else if (c.busy)` is the kneading pose (arms forward swinging at 7.5 rad/s, lean 0.2), and `liveliness` rocks any busy standing body at that rhythm. It is used for a receptionist checking someone in, a guest at a vending machine, snack machine or arcade (the `need` state sets `busy` for drink, eat and play in `guests.ts`), a Caretaker on a chore (`doingJob` sets `busy` in `staffing.ts`), and staff getting themselves a drink.
3. **Everyone lies on their back with their eyes open,** on the Massage Table ("Face hole included at no extra charge"), the Acupuncture Table, a Reiki Table, the Sound Bath Mat and the Recliner alike, arms out as if sunbathing.
4. **Nobody blinks.** The eyes are one fused shape; a face has four expressions, none for fear, so a guest on a bad trip or in an outbreak has the same at-ease mouth as the receptionist.
5. **One body.** `randomLook` varies skin (7), hair (9 colors, 8 styles: 5 for women, 3 for men), top (11), bottom (7) and a height from 0.92 to 1.08. Every shoe is the same brown, nobody wears glasses, and a crowd of forty is forty copies of the same shirt and build.
6. **A cure has no moment.** `cureRig` hides the props on the first frame the outcome is `healed`. The chicken does not fly off; it is gone. The sound (`heal`) and the bubble (`bubble-cured`) are the whole payoff for the thing the game is about. Listed as gap 31 in [two-point-gaps.md](two-point-gaps.md) and item 7 under Models in the visual audit.

Six pieces, in this order. A and B are the ones a player notices first and go first; C, D, E and F are independent of one another and of A and B, except that B, C and D all extend the cues `poseChar` is given, so B defines them.

## How to work

- **Read first:** [development.md](development.md) (how the code is laid out, the test suites, the model lab and `lab.html?model=…&still&view=…`), the paragraph on people in [architecture.md](architecture.md) (it begins "People are drawn in batches"), the guideline at the top of `src/render/models/parts.ts`, and the code named under each piece before changing it. The renderer in `src/render/` reads the sim and never writes it. `poseChar` is handed a `Char` and not the sim: anything it needs from the world is worked out once a frame in `PeopleLayer.update` (`src/render/people.ts`) and passed in, as `workStyle` and the door are today.
- **Branch from `main`** in a worktree, on one branch for the whole pass, and commit each numbered change below on its own with a lowercase Conventional Commit (`feat:`, `fix:`, `refactor:`, `test:`, `docs:`). Never push, never open a pull request: this is a solo project and the owner merges local branches. If `main` is mid-merge when you start, say so and wait.
- **The polish pass may land alongside this one.** [polish-pass.md](polish-pass.md) also touches `pose.ts` and `people.ts`: A.4 passes a list of runners to `poseChar`, B.1 passes a flood flag for a mopping Caretaker, D.1 a rattle flag. Check `git log --oneline -- src/render/pose.ts` when you start. If those commits are on `main`, keep whatever shape they gave the extra arguments and fold the `Cues` object below into it; if not, build `Cues` as written and say in the commit that the polish pass should pass its runners, flood and rattle through it.
- **Every change to how a person looks or moves gets a model-lab specimen and a picture.** Specimens live in `src/lab/catalog.ts`; the picture list is `IDS` in `tests/visual/models.spec.ts`, and `the lab shows every model, and nothing more` fails until the two agree. Take the pictures with `yarn test:visual:update` and look at every changed picture before committing it; a changed picture of something this pass did not mean to change is a bug. Pure helpers (a stride, a blink, an act) get a case in `tests/unit/body-language.test.ts`, which already builds a rig and poses it (see its `pose` and `hand` helpers).
- **Draw calls are counted, not timed.** `tests/unit/model-cost.test.ts` holds a guest to 38 calls, staff to 34 and a visitor to 35 in the lab, and `tests/perf/render.spec.ts` holds a crowd of forty to fewer than 80 calls over a few people. A part that is only shown sometimes (a cup, a wrench, a burst of sparkles) is built the first time it is needed and hidden otherwise, the way `item-cues.ts` adds a cue to an item, so a plain person costs what they cost now. Every new shape is made with `fuse` in `character-detail.ts` (one part, with a far stand-in) or `cached` in `parts.ts`, and every new material comes from `mat()`, so the crowd can batch it. A material that glows or is see-through is drawn on its own: use one only where it is the point.
- **Before finishing:** `yarn typecheck`, `yarn lint`, `yarn test`, `yarn test:perf` (the crowd's draw calls and GPU memory; it needs a free port 5184), the visual pictures, a line per piece under Unreleased in [CHANGELOG.md](CHANGELOG.md) in the voice of the lines there (A and B can share one), the "Reading people" section of [guide.md](guide.md) where the player sees a change (C, D and F), the people paragraph of [architecture.md](architecture.md) where a layer works differently (A, B, E), and tick nothing in [todo.md](todo.md) since none of this is listed there; add a line to [visual-audit.md](visual-audit.md) under Models item 6 and 7 saying what was done.
- **Words.** Comments in the game's voice: plain, short, in sentences, saying why. Every function has a one-line JSDoc comment with no tags. American English.
- **Numbers** that tune a motion live as named constants at the top of the file that uses them, with a one-line comment saying what they are for, as `PATIENCE_SIGNS`, `IDLE` and `FIDGET` do in `pose.ts`.
- **Decisions already made.** Do not re-open: the walk follows distance, not time; builds are a scale on the body, not new shapes; glasses are a look, not a symptom; nothing here changes a rule of the game or a save's meaning, and a new field on `Look` is settled for old saves from the person's id, never from the game's random numbers.

## A. Feet on the floor

**Where it is.** `poseChar` in `src/render/pose.ts` sets `const phase = t * 9.5 + c.id * 1.7` and uses it for the legs, arms, bob, knees and the hips' twist (`poseJoints` takes it through `Limbs`), and `liveliness` uses it for the walking sway. `View.render` in `src/render/view.ts` keeps `this.time` in real seconds. `Sim.tick` in `src/core/sim/sim.ts` scales by `this.s.speed`. Guests walk at 2.1 m/s, 1.75 when stiff and `RUNAWAY_SPEED` running (`guests.ts` about line 536); staff at `walk` in `staffing.ts` about line 623; visitors at 2 (`visits.ts`). The lab's people stand still at the stage's middle (`src/lab/people.ts` `base`), so a walking specimen walks on the spot.

### A.1 A stride that follows the ground

- `CharRig` (`character.ts`) gains `walked: number` (meters walked, which the stride is counted from), `lastX`, `lastY` and `lastFacing` (where they were last frame, `NaN` to begin with). Build sets `walked: 0` and the three to `NaN`.
- `pose.ts`: a pure, exported `stepOn(rig, x, y)` that adds the distance from `(lastX, lastY)` to `(x, y)` to `walked` and notes the new spot, except when the last spot is `NaN` or the jump is over `TELEPORT` (2 m: someone placed by `resetGuest`, arriving at the pavement's end, or carried by a machine's gag), which only notes the spot. `poseChar` calls it first thing, whatever the pose, so a person who stops and starts keeps their phase. Then `const phase = rig.walked * STRIDE + c.id * 1.7`, with `STRIDE = 4.8` radians a meter ("a full cycle of two steps every 1.3 m, about the 9.5 rad/s the walk had at 2.1 m/s"). Everything that read `phase` keeps reading it. The healed bounce (`Math.sin(phase * 0.5)`) comes with it.
- A runner's legs now go half as fast again as a walker's, a stiff guest's a little slower, and at triple speed everyone hurries as a sped-up film does, which is right; breathing, fidgets and the bob of a bubble stay in real time, which is also right.
- **Leaning into turns.** With `lastFacing` known, `const turning = wrap(c.facing - rig.lastFacing) / dt` (use the `wrap` already in the file; 0 when `lastFacing` is `NaN` or `dt` is 0), and while walking `side -= clamp(turning, -8, 8) * 0.02` so a body leans into a corner by up to about nine degrees, with the head leading: `headZ -= clamp(turning, -8, 8) * 0.01`. Note `lastFacing` after.
- **The lab walks on a treadmill.** `posePerson` in `src/lab/catalog.ts` (and the runaway and seated specimens, which call `poseChar` themselves) must still show the walk: export `treadmill(rig, dt, pace)` from `pose.ts`, which adds `pace * dt` to `rig.walked`, and call it before `poseChar` when the specimen's pose is `walk` at 2.1, or `RUNAWAY_SPEED` for `mood:runaway`. With `&still` the lab's `dt` is 0 and the pictures do not move, so no picture changes except by the phase offset: check `mood:runaway` and the seated pictures stay as they were, and if the legs sit differently in one, say so in the commit and accept the new picture.
- Tests (`body-language.test.ts`): a guest posed walking at `(0, 0)` then at `(0.65, 0)` has its left and right legs swapped (`rig.legL.rotation.x` changes sign; pose with `dt = 1` so the easing lands); one posed at `(0, 0)` then `(5, 0)` has not moved its legs (a jump is not a walk); `stepOn` on a fresh rig counts nothing. One for `treadmill`.
- `architecture.md`: in the people paragraph, replace "poses everyone every frame from the game state alone" with a clause saying the stride is counted from the ground covered, so the feet plant at any speed, and that the rig remembers where it was last frame for it.

## B. Doing what they do

**Where it is.** `poseChar(rig, c, t, dt, style, door)` takes a `WorkStyle` (a modality, `qigong` or null) that `PeopleLayer.workStyle` works out for a practitioner at a station. Everything else that is `busy` takes the general pose at the foot of the long `if`/`else if` chain and the 7.5 rhythm in `liveliness`. The states: a guest in `need` with `needKind` `drink`, `eat` or `play` (`busy` set in `guests.ts` `toNeed`; a `toilet` need is not busy and the guest is hidden in the restroom anyway); a receptionist at a `reception_desk` whose `it.serving` is a guest (`stations.ts` sets `staff.busy = !!served` about line 262; the guest being checked in stands at the desk in `called` or `served`); a member of staff in `doingJob` on a `Job` of kind `litter`, `water`, `repair`, `clean`, `launder`, `install` or `refresh` (`Job` in `src/core/state/types.ts`; `smudge` already has its own pose, `soul` and `ward` theirs, `calm` is a chase); a guest at a shop counter (`goal === 'shop'`, served at the counter: find it in `stations.ts` about line 327, where `bubble-shopping` is said).

Callers: `people.ts`, `src/lab/catalog.ts` (five places), `tests/unit/body-language.test.ts`. No behavior changes in this commit; every picture stays the same.
- `PeopleLayer.update` builds the cues once per person: `style` as now, `door` as now, `act` from `actOf(c)` (a new pure, exported function in `people.ts` or a small `src/render/acts.ts`, given the char and the item they are at, from `this.sim.world.item(c.claimItem)` for a guest in `need`, `c.post` for a receptionist, `c.job` for staff): a guest in `need` → their `needKind` (`drink`, `eat`, `play`); a guest `served` or `called` at an item whose `ITEMS` def has `goal === 'shop'` → `shop`; a receptionist `atPost` and `busy` → `checkin`; staff `doingJob` and `busy` with job kind `litter` or `clean` or `launder` → `litter`, `water` → `water`, `repair` or `install` → `repair`, `refresh` → `drink` (or `eat` if `wants(st) === 'eat'`, the helper `staffing.ts` uses to pick the bubble; import it). `prone` is C's and stays `false` here.

### B.2 The acts, one commit each or two to a commit

Each act replaces the general busy pose in `poseChar` (`if (cues.act) …` before the `c.busy` chain, and `liveliness` returns breathing alone when an act is set, so the body does not rock at 7.5) and, where it holds something, adds a prop to the rig built on first use: `rig.held?: { cup?: Object3D; snack?: Object3D; wrench?: Object3D; can?: Object3D }`, each hung from `rig.joints.foreR` at `-0.31 + ELBOW` (where the sage bundle hangs, see `buildCharacter`), shown only while its act is on. Build each with `put` and `mat` in a new `src/render/models/held.ts`, one fused or `cached` shape where it can be one part, no shadows (`castShadow = false`).

1. **`checkin`.** Forearms level as if on the keyboard: `armL = armR = -1.0`, elbows `-1.35` (set through `life.elbowL/R` the way `passTime` does with `bendTo`), hands a little in (`bendL = bendR = 0.3`), the fingers going: `elbowL += sin(t * 11 + id) * 0.05`, `elbowR += sin(t * 11 + id + 1.6) * 0.05`. Head up to the guest with a nod now and then: `headX = 0.05 + max(0, sin(t * 1.4 + id)) * 0.14`. No lean, no rock. Picture: `act:checkin` (a receptionist at a reception desk, with the guest at the desk's guest spot, built like the `seated:` specimens from the item's `access`).
2. **`drink` and `eat`.** A cup (a `cyl(0.035, 0.028, 0.09, 10)` in cream with a dark brown disc on top, one fused shape) or a snack (an `rbox(0.09, 0.05, 0.03, 0.01)` in `0xe5484d` red with a cream band, one part) in the right hand. The arm cycles every `SIP = 2.4` s: up to the mouth (`armR = -2.05`, elbow `-2.3`, `headX = -0.12`) for the middle third, down to `-0.7` otherwise, eased with `sin`; eating adds a chew, `headX += sin(t * 9) * 0.02`, while the hand is up. The left arm hangs. Staff on `refresh` use the same. Pictures: `act:drink`, `act:eat` (a guest alone, the hand up).
3. **`play`.** Both hands forward on the controls: `armL = armR = -1.15`, elbows `-1.4`, lean `0.15`, head down `0.2`, a quick alternate jab `elbowL += max(0, sin(t * 10 + id)) * 0.18` and the right with `+ π`, the body swaying with the game `side = sin(t * 2.6 + id) * 0.06`. 4. **`shop`.** One hand up on the counter (`armR = -0.95`, elbow `-1.2`), the other pointing at the shelf (`armL = -1.7`, elbow `-0.3`, `armInL` 0.2), head turned to it (`headYaw = 0.5`) then back. Picture: `act:shop`.
5. **`litter`.** A stoop and a pick, every `PICK = 1.8` s: lean to `0.95` with knees `0.5` and `bodyY -0.16`, the right arm reaching down (`armR = 0.35`, elbow `-0.15`), the left back for balance (`armL = 0.4`); then straight with the right arm at `-0.6` for the toss, eased with `sin`. Also for `clean` and `launder`, which are wiping and lifting; one pose will do. Picture: `act:litter` (at the stoop).
6. **`water`.** A watering can (a `cyl` body, a `cyl` spout turned forward, a torus handle, one fused shape, green `0x5a8a3f`) in the right hand, arm forward `-0.9` and the can tipping on the wrist (`foreR.rotation.z` is taken by `bendR`; tip it through the prop's own `rotation.x = 0.3 + sin(t * 1.2) * 0.25`), lean `0.15` toward the plant, head down `0.15`. Picture: `act:water`.
7. **`repair`.** A crouch: knees `0.95`, `bodyY -0.22`, lean `0.45`, head down `0.25`; a wrench (a `cyl(0.012, 0.012, 0.22)` grey with an `rbox` jaw, one fused shape) in the right hand working at hip height: `armR = -0.75`, elbow `-0.6`, a twist every half second `foreR.rotation.y` can be set through `bendR = 0.2 + sin(t * 4 + id) * 0.35`. The left hand steadying on the knee (`armL = -0.3`, elbow `-0.9`). Also for `install`. Picture: `act:repair`.

## C. Lying as they would

## D. Faces that blink

**Where it is.** `detailHead` in `character-detail.ts` puts `eyes()` and `glints()` as two parts named `eyes` and `glints`, and a `Face` holding the mouth and brows whose geometry `showExpression` swaps between the four expressions' fused shapes, so every face with one expression batches together. `expressionOf` in `pose.ts` picks the expression.

### D.1 A blink

- `character-detail.ts`: `eyesShut()`, a fused shape of two short pills lying along where the eyes are (`pill(0.006, 0.03)` at `EYE.x`, `EYE.y - 0.004`, on `faceZ`, turned a little as the eyes are), in the eyes' dark material. `Face` gains `eyes: THREE.Mesh`, `glints: THREE.Mesh` and `shut: boolean`; `showEyes(face, shut)` swaps `eyes.geometry` between `eyes()` and `eyesShut()` and sets `glints.visible = !shut`, doing nothing when unchanged.
- `pose.ts`: `blinkAt(id, t)`, pure and exported: `const period = 3.2 + hash(id) * 2.2; const s = t + id * 0.73; return s % period < BLINK` with `BLINK = 0.13` s. A doubled blink now and then is not worth it. `poseChar` calls `showEyes(rig.face, asleep || blinkAt(c.id, t))` where `asleep` is C.2's: a guest `served` and `lie` and not prone, or `resting`. Anyone `lie` in a machine's gag is hidden or spun and does not matter.
- The lab holds `&still` at `t = 0` and `dt = 0`; `blinkAt(1, 0)` must be false for the lab's one specimen id, so no picture changes: assert it in the test. Add a lab control on guests, `toggle('Eyes shut', st, 'shut')`, and a picture `face:shut` under `FACES` (the head view).
- Test: over a long run of `t`, `blinkAt` is true about `BLINK / period` of the time for a few ids, and never for two whole seconds in a row.

### D.2 A frightened face

- `Expression` gains `'scared'`: brows high and lifted at the inner ends (`BROWS.scared = { y: 0.096, dip: 0.5 }`, so they tilt as a wince does but sit higher), and a mouth that is a small open oval: in `mouth()`, for `scared` a `ball(0.024, n(14))` scaled `[0.8, 1.15, 0.5]` set into the face at `MOUTH_Y - 0.004`, in the mouth's material (it reads as a dark O).
- `expressionOf`: a guest `running`, or `stressed`, is `scared` before anything but `healed`; staff about to quit stay `unhappy`.
- Lab: `FACES` in `catalog.ts` and `models.spec.ts` gain `['scared', 'Frightened, in an outbreak or on a bad trip']`, and `feel()` sets `c.stressed = true` for it; the `mood:runaway` picture changes to the new face, which is meant.

## F. The cure's moment

**Where it is.** `cureRig` in `character.ts` sets `rig.cured`, hides every `props` entry, resets the head's scale and the skin. `poseChar` calls it on the first frame `c.outcome === 'healed'`, and `stations.ts` (about line 410) sets the outcome, says `bubble-cured` for 6 s and plays `heal`. A healed guest walking bounces (`bodyY += |sin(phase/2)| × 0.07`). `src/render/gags/particles.ts` has emitters for the machines, which are per-item parts; a person's burst is better as one shape.

### F.1 The symptom goes in a puff

- `CharRig` gains `curedAt: number` (`NaN` until healed) and `burst?: THREE.Object3D`. `cureRig(rig, look, t)` sets `curedAt = t` and no longer hides anything; the hiding moves to `poseChar`, which each frame while `t - curedAt < CURE = 0.8` s eases `u = (t - curedAt) / CURE`: every prop in `rig.props` scales to `(1 - u)` of its built scale (note each prop's built scale on the rig the first time, as `gags/apply.ts` keeps a `rest`) and rises `0.25 × u`; the head eases from `bighead`'s 1.45 back to 1; the skin material swaps back at `u > 0.5`.
