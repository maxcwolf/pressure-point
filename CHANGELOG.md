# Changelog

Each release's notes are taken from its section here. Keep them short.

## Unreleased

- **Objectives show their numbers.** Click an objective for what counts behind it: heals and failures this month and last, the sample a monthly goal needs, the money and the loan behind a balance goal, a room's prestige terms, standing points by source, and how far each is from being met.
- **Two warnings before a line costs reputation.** A toast names the line, the count and the levers as its first waiting guest's condition turns poor, and a louder one names each guest within two days of going home worse off, with the advisor on it too. Lines and the guest inspector show the days of condition left, and the floor can be colored by it.
- **Payday in plain sight.** The Money menu's new Payroll tab shows the wages due against the bank, the loan room and this month's projected net, with a verdict in a line. The payday warning comes as soon as the month's pace says payday is not covered, with the numbers, and again five days before.
- **No dialog lost.** A star, an award or a visit's report that arrives at the same moment as another now waits its turn instead of being lost, with a payday or a bankruptcy shown first. A failed inspection's choice between the fine and making it go away no longer closes on Esc, and is shown again after loading the game.
- **Karma at once.** Karma from a star or an achievement can be spent in the karma shop straight away, not only after the next autosave.
- **Previous game kept safe.** Opening "Previous game" keeps it saved from the start, rather than only from its first autosave.
- **Older saves' patience.** Months from saves made before patience was kept no longer count as guests keeping none of it.
- **Fairer goals and awards.** Guests put off by the prices no longer count toward the month's waits and patience. The Exchange's rush-hour tempers are short only during a rush. A visitor who could reach no room on their tour leaves no verdict.
- **House calls at today's prices.** A house call is paid at the prices of the day the practitioner gets back, not those of the moment it was taken.
- **Classes booked around a drink.** An Instructor off for a drink or a snack as a class is booked no longer empties it; only a break, course or house call that would keep them away past the class does.
- **Rules that work as described.** No emergency guests arrive while High Pines is snowed in. Mushrooms put away for an inspector who never came are back on sale. At The Orchard Estate, land and upgrade levels open at their stars, not from the start. A Brisk Caretaker or Shaman works faster, and a Chatterbox slower. The nudge to fill a qualification slot comes at every rank. A Sound Healer's Bedside Manner helps guests waiting at the mats. Someone who cheers up before a Shaman comes can be helped again later. A heal's reputation goes by how patient the guest still was.
- **Templates and rugs.** A Qi Gong Studio template is locked while classes are switched off, a template with too few of a required item cannot be built, and a moved or copied room's rug can no longer land on a rug in the corridor.
- **Clearer numbers.** A guest's inspector shows what their treatment will cost at your prices, and the Staff menu leaves out staff who are leaving and shows what the next payday owes. The Instructor's and the inspector's descriptions now match what they do.

## v0.30.14

- **Class-goers get their class.** Far fewer Qi Gong class-goers go home disappointed. With class-goers waiting, the Instructor holds off a drink or a snack instead of leaving the mat and sending everyone back to the line. Anyone in early for a later class no longer takes a spot from someone due before them, and anyone still in line when a class ends is booked into the next one. The walk in to the studio no longer wears on their patience.
- **Calm a class-goer.** During a stress outbreak, a class-goer's inspector has the Calm button too.
- **Amanda keeps pace.** When Amanda Jablonsky applies, she now always outranks the other applicants of the day.
- **Smoother crowds.** People in the distance are drawn more cheaply, so a busy center runs more smoothly.

## v0.30.13

- **Seats that fit.** People sitting on benches, sofas, chairs and toilets no longer have their legs pass through the seat, and staff sitting on the floor now sit properly cross-legged.
- **A new Massage Chair.** The Massage Chair has been redesigned as a padded shell with a calf rest, shoulder wings and a remote on the arm.

## v0.30.12

- **Livelier, more detailed people.** Everyone now has knees and elbows, a face with eyes, brows and a smile, finer hair, and shoes with soles. They shift their weight as they walk, breathe and fidget while they wait, and lean into their work. Every uniform has been refitted to match.
- **Items you can't afford.** The Items menu now refuses an item you can't afford and says how much more you need, and buttons that work no longer look faded.

## v0.30.9

## v0.30.8

- **Autosave every 5 minutes.** A new player's game now autosaves every 5 minutes rather than every 12 seconds, and still saves whenever you leave it, close the window or switch away. A choice already made in Settings is kept.
- **A loaded game carries on as it was.** Saving and loading no longer changes what happens next: the game's luck, the day's practice and the feel of each room are saved with it.
- **Saves kept safe on desktop.** The app keeps a copy of every save in files of its own, and brings them back if its storage is ever lost. A save that fails partway leaves the last good one in place.
- **Full screen on launch.** The app reliably reopens in full screen when you left it that way, even when it starts behind another window.
- **Smoother building.** Checking where something can go while you hold it is about seven times quicker.

## v0.30.7

- **A quieter Qi Gong call at speed.** The announcement that a class is starting is now heard as often at 2x and 3x speed as at normal speed, instead of two or three times as often.
- **A tidier Cancel course button.** It now sits under a Training heading with the staff inspector's other actions and looks like them.

## v0.30.6

- **Cancel a course.** A member of staff on a course has a Cancel course button in their inspector. After you confirm, they go back to work and the whole fee is refunded. They don't gain the qualification.

## v0.30.5

- **Experts learn every treatment.** An Expert can now study any treatment of their discipline even with every qualification slot filled, so a Shaman, or anyone who took a general course on the way up, is no longer shut out of their last one. General courses still need a free slot.

## v0.30.4

- **Qi Gong classes keep their class-goers.** A third or more of class-goers used to miss their class, costing reputation, so a studio lost money. Places are now booked only while the Instructor is at their mat, latecomers are put down for the next class, and class-goers no longer count against the guest limit. A studio now pays its way.
- **Nobody walled in.** Building an item, room or door can no longer seal someone into a pocket of the building they can't walk out of.
- **Full screen that stays.** The desktop app has a Full screen switch under Settings › Display, and remembers it between launches.
- **Ask before letting staff go.** Let go in the staff inspector now asks "Are you sure?" with their name first.

## v0.30.3

- **Smaller Qi Gong classes.** An Instructor now leads at most 12, from 6 as a Trainee (6, 8, 9, 11 and 12 by rank), so a class ending no longer floods the shop and the restrooms. A place costs a little more, $130 at standard prices.

## v0.30.2

- **No more vanishing on the walk to the orangery.** At The Orchard, guests walking between the main building and a building of its own no longer shrink away halfway along the pavement and grow back. People now arrive and leave past the far building, at the edge of the lot.
- **Shorter shop lines.** A guest on their way out passes the shop by if every Shop Counter already has five waiting, instead of joining a line that filled the building after a big Qi Gong class. A sale is also quicker, so each counter serves more guests.

## v0.30.1

- **Posture scans are worth having.** A guest sent to the Posture Lab now has a 10% better chance of their treatment working, shown as "📐 Scanned" in their inspector, and a scan costs $250. The lab only sees guests a junior Consultant is unsure about, so it pays off with junior Consultants on staff.

## v0.30.0

- **Waits you can read.** Goals about waiting now ask guests to keep a share of their **patience** through their visit, the lowest their patience bar falls, rather than an average wait in days. The Stats chart is now **Patience kept**, and the No Waiting Around award is judged on it. The targets are set to be as hard as before.
- **Staff see their line first.** With guests waiting at their table, staff hold off on a drink or a snack until they're properly thirsty or hungry, and leave bad vibes in other rooms until the line is seen to. Waits are a little shorter and fewer guests storm out.
- **Long lines put guests off.** Once more than three guests wait for each practitioner of a discipline, some new guests hear of it and stay away. The Stats › Guests tab shows each discipline's line and how many it keeps away.
- **Find what's holding guests up.** When guests pile up, the advisor names the slowest stage (check-in, consultation or a treatment) and why: too few staff, staff called away, or a station busy nearly all the time. A desk or table's inspector shows how busy it's been and how often someone was at it.

## v0.29.1

- **Class-goers find a studio in a building of its own.** A Qi Gong Studio on land apart from the main building, like The Orchard's orangery, no longer has its class-goers walk to the main front door and all the way round, arriving after the class. They come along the pavement from the nearer end and go straight to it.

## v0.29.0

- **Guests come for what you do well.** Each new guest picks a discipline by how popular it is, its **following** (which grows with every guest it heals and shrinks with every one it lets down), its finest room and whether your staff are trained for it. A new discipline starts with a small following and grows as word gets round, and each discipline you staff past the first brings a few more guests in all. The Stats menu has a new **Guests** tab showing each discipline's share of new guests, and why.
- **Practitioners by rank.** A Qi Gong Instructor leads a class of 6 as a Trainee, up to 16 as an Expert, and a Sound Healer plays for 2 mats as a Trainee, up to 4 as an Expert. Spots and mats past that stand empty until they're promoted.
- **Classes that go ahead.** A Qi Gong class runs about a day, so one no longer runs into the next. The Instructor stays to the end of a class, rests after Friday's rather than just before Monday's, and is back in time. When class-goers go home disappointed, you're told why, including when every spot was taken.
- **The Sound Healer plays out the session** for everyone lying down before going for a break, instead of walking off halfway.
- **Fairer staff challenges.** A challenge to heal guests in one discipline asks for that discipline's share of the guests, not a fixed part of them all.

## v0.17.0
