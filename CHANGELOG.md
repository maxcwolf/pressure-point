# Changelog

Each release's notes are taken from its section here. Keep them short.

## Unreleased

- **A worsening condition:** every guest has a Condition bar that falls while they wait, faster with some conditions (Stress Head, Flat Battery, Gull Shock) and slower with others. The lower it gets, the less likely their treatment is to work, and if it runs out they go home worse off: a one-star review and a big knock to reputation. Emergency guests arrive already in a bad way. A 🤕 shows over anyone in poor shape.
- **Bad vibes:** a failed treatment leaves a gloomy cloud over its room. Treatments there work less often and staff working there are unsettled, until a Reiki Practitioner, or a Janitor with the new Smudging course, walks the room wafting a smoldering bundle of sage.
- **Stress outbreaks** (from the second star): offered like an emergency. Stressed guests sweat and fidget, and stress spreads to anyone waiting close by. Click the ones you spot and calm them with the few sessions you have; if few enough leave still stressed, it pays a bonus.
- **Got worse** is counted in the casebook, the Stats charts and the Money menu.

## v0.12.0

- **A Shop Counter** (from the first star): books, crystals, oils and candles for guests to take home. Treated guests stop on their way out, more often and for more the better their visit went, and a receptionist serves them. Keep a receptionist to the front desk or the shop under Duties.
- **Prices per treatment:** each treatment has its own price on top of the overall level, with how many it has put off, so a popular treatment can cost more while the everyday ones stay cheap.
- **Marketing:** campaigns for word of mouth, for a condition in the spotlight (three times as many guests with it), or for recruitment (an applicant a day, and better ones), bought for one to three months.
- **House calls:** now and then someone asks for a practitioner to come to them for a day or more, sometimes trained in a particular treatment. Whoever goes is away until they are back, and the fee rises with their rank. The Exchange gets the most.
- **The Money menu has tabs:** Books, Prices, Marketing and Loan, and the books show shop sales, house calls and marketing.
- **The version is shown on the title screen.**
- **Placing furniture shows who works it:** the square a member of staff stands on shows their role's icon, and the hint names the role.
- **Fixed:** rush hours at The Exchange lasted about a second each and chimed every few seconds. They now come in waves of two days in every six, the same share of the time, with a tip the first time instead of an announcement each time.

## v0.11.0

- **Guest feelings:** guests notice what pleases and annoys them (a long wait, nothing to drink, a filthy toilet, litter, a lovely spot to wait, a comfy seat, an untrained practitioner, steep prices, a beautiful room) and their inspector lists it.
- **Reviews and reputation in parts:** every guest leaves a star rating and a line about their visit. Click the reputation figure, or open Stats, to see the latest reviews and how the center does on results, experience, value, staff and word of mouth. The overall reputation works as before.
- **A casebook:** a Stats tab showing every visit by condition, with how many were healed, not healed, stormed out, sent home or put off by the price, and the cure rate.
- **Overlays:** the 🗺️ button at the top right colors the floor by how pleasant each spot is, guests' patience or needs, staff energy, or the upkeep of the furniture.
- **The Stats menu has tabs:** Charts, Reputation, Casebook and Awards.
- **Every announcement is now voiced,** with five new recordings, and three new familiar names among guests and applicants. Amanda Jablonsky now and then applies as a massage therapist.
- **Fixed:** decor counted for nothing until a second of play had passed, so straight after loading a game, and all the while it was paused, the surroundings were treated as bare.

## v0.10.0

- **Staff careers:** staff earn experience from the guests they see and the jobs they do, and rise through five ranks, from Trainee to Expert, in place of skill stars. When ready they wait on a card at the side for a promotion, which brings the raise they insist on; putting it off sours them. Each rank brings a qualification slot, and every slot must be filled by training before the next promotion.
- **General courses:** Bedside Manner, Motivation and Stamina Training for anyone, Customer Service for receptionists and Maintenance for Janitors. They take the place of the old skill course, and each fills a slot.
- **Duties:** keep a consultant to consultations or to the Posture Lab, or a Janitor from any of their jobs, from the staff inspector.
- **A hiring pool that grows:** a new applicant turns up every few days rather than a fresh batch each month, up to six per role as stars are earned, and an applicant can be turned down to make room.
- **Five new traits you can see:** a Litterbug drops litter, a Grump sends guests off less pleased, a Ray of Sunshine cheers colleagues nearby, a Quick Study gets through courses faster, and a Green Thumb waters thirsty plants in passing.

## v0.9.0

- **Staff challenges:** now and then a member of staff sets the center an objective with a deadline of two to four months, such as healing more guests, a better cure rate, a finer room or nobody quitting. It shows under the star objectives; meet it in time for a cash reward and a happier team. The sandbox can switch them off.
- **Year-end awards:** at the end of each year the center is judged on the year just gone, for eight awards from Healing Hands and Fair Prices to Healer of the Year and Janitor of the Year. Each won pays out and adds to reputation, a member of staff who wins one is delighted, and the bar rises every year. Trophies won fill the Trophy Cabinet's shelves and are listed under Awards in the Stats menu.

## v0.8.0

- **A Policy menu:** set how sure a diagnosis must be before treatment (75% to start), call emergency guests ahead of everyone else, choose the energy at which staff take a break, and cap how many of each role may be on a break at once.
- **Lines you can manage:** a station's inspector lists who is waiting, with their patience and chance of success, and buttons to move each guest up or down, over to the shortest other line of the same kind, or home.
- **Chances shown:** a diagnosed guest's inspector gives their treatment's chance of success, and why it is low when it is.
- **Send a guest home** from their inspector or the line: they leave untreated, pay no treatment fee, and your reputation takes a small knock, less than a failed treatment. Sent home is counted in the Stats menu.
- **Fixed:** an emergency's days left could read a day too many, when subtracting one part day from another came out a hair over a whole number.
- **Priority rooms:** switch Priority on in a room's inspector and staff fill its stations first, leaving a quieter post to do it, and Janitors see to it before anywhere else.
- **Raise requests wait at the side:** each open request has its own card at the top right until it is answered, and clicking it selects the person asking. The advisor stays free for tips and goals.
- **The announcer mixes it up:** a filler line is not said again until at least half the others have been.
- **Tidier front lawn and title screen:** the sign out front no longer has a leg in the building's wall, and the save card on the title screen keeps clear of the panel's edges, wrapping a long date rather than spilling over.
- **Fixed:** five rare cases found in the October audit, among them staff on their way out being put back to work and emergency guests counted as sent when they could not get in. The desktop app also turns down permission checks and connects to nothing but itself, and saves now carry a new version number, so older versions of the game refuse them rather than crash.

## v0.7.2

- **Resize a built room:** Resize in a room's inspector lets you drag any wall in or out. The door and windows move with their walls, the furniture has to stay inside, and floor added costs $200 a tile, with half back for floor given up.

## v0.7.1

- **The title screen fits the window:** it sizes itself to the window's height, and Campaign, Sandbox, Load game and Settings now open in place of the menu under the logo, with a Back button (or Esc), scrolling within themselves when they are long.
- **A proper macOS icon:** the app's icon is now a rounded square of its own, with the PPI card filling it, rather than a small card that macOS shrank onto a gray tile.
- **Rooms against the building's walls** use those walls instead of standing a second wall in front of them. Their windows are set into the building's wall, and the building's own windows make way.
- **Loans no longer count toward a bank balance goal,** so a star cannot be bought with borrowed money and paid back.
- **Running out of storage is no longer silent:** a failed autosave says so, once, and a new game is refused rather than started when there is no room to keep the old one.
- **Smoother autosaves:** the title screen's picture of the game is taken only on the way out, rather than at every autosave, which stalled a frame.
- **Fixed:** an emergency under way is called off, not failed, when its treatment is switched off; the desktop app runs one copy at a time, and a browser tab warns when another tab saves over its game; the game saves as the page is hidden, so a phone or Safari closing it loses nothing; a cancelled drag no longer leaves the camera panning; W, A, S and D pan on any keyboard layout; and the same save file can be imported twice.

## v0.7.0

- **A campaign:** Campaign on the title screen leads to a trail of centers, each with its own building, goals and guests. Start at Willow Lane, a small massage-only unit; a star there opens The Exchange downtown, with acupuncture, office ailments and rush hours. Earned stars are kept between games.
- **Saltmarsh,** the third center: a seaside town with seasons (busy summers, quiet winters), seaside ailments, sea air that wears equipment out faster, and reiki.
- **Karma:** campaign stars and eight achievements earn karma, spent in the karma shop or from the Items menu on items that stay unlocked in every center. It unlocks comfort and decor early, and three new items found nowhere else: a Massage Chair, a Bonsai and a Koi Pond.
- **Sandbox** on the title screen starts the open-ended game, as New game did before, now with settings for the starting money, the treatments on offer, and whether everything is unlocked from the start.
- **The announcer speaks:** some announcements are now read out over the center's speakers, after the chime, with the music turned down while they talk.

## v0.6.0

- **Messages take you there:** click a message or the advisor about someone or something, and the camera glides over with it selected.
- **Emergency offers can wait:** put one aside and it shrinks to a button with the days left to decide; press it to accept or decline. The offer itself now shows its countdown too.
- **Items menu filters:** by where an item goes (the corridors or a room) and what it is for (equipment, guest comforts, decor, things for staff).
- **A Stats menu:** charts of guests, outcomes, waiting times, money and reputation, month by month for a year. Choose which to show; the choice is remembered.
- **Guests put off by prices** are now counted on their own, and shown in the Money menu beside the price controls.
- **Morale at a glance:** in the Staff menu beside energy, and a last warning two days before someone quits: an alarm, a ⚠️ over their head and a countdown.
- **Fixed:** a batch of bugs found in an audit, among them refunds for torn-up rooms, saves that could not be read, staff vanishing as they quit and keyboard shortcuts firing on key repeat. The game also runs more smoothly, with lighter trees and music that costs nothing while switched off.

## v0.5.0

- **Fixed:** an acupuncture emergency no longer asks for "Cupping massage" and the like.
